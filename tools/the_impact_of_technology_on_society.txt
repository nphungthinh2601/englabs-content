In today’s rapidly evolving world, technology has become an inseparable part of our daily lives. It has changed the way we communicate, work, and even think. The advent of the internet and smartphones, in particular, has transformed how individuals interact with each other and access information. Technology has greatly influenced various sectors, including education, healthcare, and business, enabling greater efficiency and convenience. However, the effects of technology on society are not without controversy, as it has also led to new challenges and concerns.
